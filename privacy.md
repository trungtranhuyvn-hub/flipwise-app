# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Flipwise does not collect any data. Everything you create stays on your Mac.

## What the app stores

Your clock settings (theme, size, display options) and the countdowns and anniversaries you add.

## Where it is stored

Only on your Mac, inside the app's own container and an App Group shared with the Flipwise widgets. There is no server, no account and no cloud sync.

## Who can see it

Only you. The developer has no server and cannot see your data.

## No tracking, analytics or sharing

Flipwise has no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party SDKs. Nothing is sold or shared.

## Notifications

Reminders for your events are scheduled locally on your Mac and are not sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Flipwise Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the developer does not receive your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store whether Pro is unlocked.

## Deleting your data

Delete an event in the app at any time. Removing Flipwise from your Mac removes all of its data.

## Children

Flipwise is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
